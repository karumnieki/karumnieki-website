Cookie policy

Updated 4 October 2026.

This policy explains what karumnieki.lv stores on your device and why. The controller is SIA “SunBee”. Contact details and your rights are described in the privacy policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a site stores in your browser. Browser storage (localStorage) works in a similar way. Anything that the site does not need in order to work may be used only with your consent.

Without your consent

Until the “Allow” button is pressed, the site loads no Google tool and sends nothing to Google. If you press “Decline”, the site works just the same. Scrolling the page does not count as consent.

Where the data goes

Google Ireland Limited processes Google Analytics data on our behalf. It may also reach Google LLC in the USA, which has joined the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Google Analytics does not store the IP addresses of users in the European Union.
