About us

Kārumnieki guest house: a place where relaxation becomes an adventure

Kārumnieki is a cosy, warm guest house right on the edge of the forest. Here nature meets homeliness, and the everyday rush stays outside the door.

Everyone feels at home at Kārumnieki, whatever their age, their line of work or the weather. Joy, smiles, conversation and jokes fill the place, and in spring a special “loud silence” rings all around: birdsong that reminds you how close nature is.

Here everyone can find their own way to relax and enjoy time with the people who matter to them.

Want to throw a fun party for your friends?
Kārumnieki is a place to laugh, celebrate, listen to music and lose yourself in the evening.

Planning an event for family or friends?
Space, nature, a homely atmosphere and the chance to be together make Kārumnieki a wonderful place for a celebration or simply a lovely break.

Kārumnieki is a place where there is no need to hurry.
A place where you can be loud and merry, or quiet and calm.
A place where the forest is right outside the door and cosiness waits inside.

Come and visit us at Kārumnieki: there is always room for a good mood here!

Celebrations and events

A hall for up to 45 guests

On the ground floor there is a hall for up to 45 guests with its own kitchen. The tables can stand in one long row or be spread across the whole hall, so it hosts weddings, milestone birthdays, seminars, corporate events and team games.

By arrangement we provide catering, musicians and an event host. There is a fireplace at the end of the hall, and beyond the big windows are birches and a meadow.

Overnight stays

Upstairs, for a proper rest

Upstairs there are bedrooms with beds and floor mattresses, where you can get a proper night’s sleep after a long day. After the party there is no need to drive home straight away, because we will have the beds made up for you!

There are also showers here and a red bathroom with a jacuzzi. Bed linen is provided.

We will also take care of good music, sweet dreams and other surprises.

Sauna and hot tub

A wood-fired sauna on the ground floor, a hot tub outside

You can warm dew-soaked feet by the fireplace.

  • The sauna is a wood-fired Latvian pirts on the ground floor, with a lounge and a shower next to it
  • The hot tub stands on its own deck outside, looking out over the house and the pond
  • The sauna and the hot tub are booked together with an event or an overnight stay

Grounds

A pond, a meadow and a sports field

By the house there is a pond where the sun is mirrored by day and the moon by night. In the summer heat you can cool off in it, and in the evenings listen to the frogs’ chorus.

  • A pond you can swim in, a meadow and birches around the house
  • A barbecue and a fire pit by the shed
  • A volleyball court and football goals
  • Festoon lights in the evening
  • Space for outdoor events and a tent
